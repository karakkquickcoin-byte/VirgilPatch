VirgilPatch Updater 1.3.0

1. Extract VirgilUpdater.exe to a normal folder.
2. Run it and choose your Epsilon _retail_\Patches folder if needed.
3. Close WoW, then click Update.

If antivirus reports a threat, stop and keep the file blocked or quarantined.
Do not disable protection or add an antivirus exclusion.
Report the URL, threat name, affected file path and ZIP SHA-256 to the developer.
