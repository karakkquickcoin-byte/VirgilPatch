Chronicles of Lordaeron Launcher 1.5.1

1. Extract VirgilUpdater.exe into a normal folder you can write to.
2. Run it, click Select folder, and choose your Epsilon _retail_\Patches folder if needed.
3. Close WoW, then click Update. Select only the patch folders you want.
4. Review What's new / repair preview, then click Update / repair selected.

Existing broken or interrupted patches are repaired automatically in selected folders. Matching assets are
reused; missing/corrupt files download individually. Missing patch.json is restored after its files verify.
Unselected folders stay untouched. Progress shows %, transfer size and estimated time remaining.
Cancel keeps completed files for retry. Close an old stuck updater and reopen before trying the repaired release.
The first check reads existing selected files locally once to discard stale caches. Deep repair repeats that
verification when needed; it does not force a full download. Subsequent normal checks use verified caches.

Drag any edge or the bottom-right corner to resize the launcher.
Guides & Links opens the Chronicles guides, registers, wiki and the Watch2gether music room in your browser.
The Epsilon phase ID (35602) is shown on the launcher; click it to copy.

The launcher keeps itself up to date: every time you open it, it checks for a newer signed launcher and,
if one is published, installs it and restarts by itself. No redownload is needed.
Patch files are different: they are only checked and changed when you click Update.
It keeps a previous-version backup and restores it if the new program fails to start.
Users on v1.3.x or earlier must replace their EXE once to enable automatic program upgrades.

If antivirus reports a threat, keep the file blocked/quarantined and report the exact detection.
Do not disable protection or add an antivirus exclusion. Release signatures are not an antivirus clearance.

The launcher artwork is embedded in the EXE; no separate image download is needed.

Ambience starts at 40% volume and repeats while the launcher is open.
Click Sound on (40%) / Sound off at the bottom right to mute or unmute.
The audio is embedded in the EXE; no separate audio download is needed.

The falling-leaves background is an embedded silent 12-second loop; it adds no extra audio.
It pauses while minimized and uses a matching still image if the video cannot play.
All 1.5.0 repair, selected-folder, progress and safety behavior remains intact.
